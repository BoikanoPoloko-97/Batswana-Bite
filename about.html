About Batswana Bites

Batswana Bites is dedicated to promoting, documenting and sharing the rich culinary heritage of Botswana. The website features traditional foods together with information about their ingredients, preparation methods and cultural importance. It's purpose is to make Botswana traditional food knowledge accessible online.

Goal

The main goal of Batswana Bites is to promote Botswana's traditional food heritage through digital media, to encourage young people to learn and appreciate their cultural value.

Vision

To become a trusted platform for promoting Botswana's culinary heritage locally and internationally.
